Biography

More than Mora
It’s Gabriel Armando

Gabriel Mora Quintero turns his experiences into songs that connect with those who listen. With a voice of his own and a mix of urban sounds and emotional melodies, his music speaks of love, growth and everything that marks us.

Each release is a new chapter; each stage, a way of sharing his story.
